## The problem

Altabrio was weeks from launching a training product, which is the worst moment to
discover that the navigation does not hold. Everything was built, and what it needed was
not more features. It needed to be legible to someone opening it for the first time.

## The way in

The first thing anyone meets is the sign in, so it does the introducing. What the product
is sits beside the form rather than on a page before it, so nobody has to read a pitch to
get to their account.

## The dashboard

A training product generates a lot of numbers, and the temptation is to show all of them.
The layout puts the few that answer "how am I doing" in front, and keeps the rest one move
away rather than on the same screen competing with them.

## Result

Navigation streamlined, the visual identity rebuilt, and a product that could go out on
time and be understood by the people who signed up first.
