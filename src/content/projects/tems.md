## The problem

A logistics app has two people in it who want opposite things. A sender wants a price, a
pickup and to know where their parcel is. A rider wants work, a route, and to get paid for
it. Both of them were going into one product.

## Pick a side first

The screen straight after the splash asks which one you are, because almost nothing after
that point is shared between them. Deciding it once, at the front, is what keeps the rest
of the app from having to ask again on every screen.

<img src="/images/projects/tems-3.avif" alt="Choosing an account type, sender or rider, on the screen after the splash" width="720" height="450" loading="lazy" decoding="async" />

## Two dashboards

From there it is two products. Booking is a short form rather than a long one, because
somebody standing over a parcel is not going to fill in a page. Tracking and delivery are
the two things a sender opens the app for after that, so they are the two things the
dashboard leads with.

## Result

An end-to-end experience mapped for both sides, and a visual system underneath it built to
be added to rather than redrawn every time a screen is needed.
