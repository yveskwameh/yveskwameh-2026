## What they had

Scroll House Studio makes cinematic property films for estate agents in South Florida. Drone
footage, interior walkthroughs, colour grading, custom soundtracks.

That is a product you cannot judge from a description. An agent deciding whether to spend
money on a film for a listing wants to see a film, and every second they spend reading about
the films is a second the work is not playing.

They also did not need a big site. One studio, one service, one kind of buyer. They brought
me in to design and build it.

## One page

Everything sits on a single page, in the order the decision actually goes: see the work,
understand what you get, ask for it.

The hero is footage rather than a headline over a stock photograph, so the first thing you
judge is the thing you would be paying for. The explanation comes second, once there is
something to explain, and it names the parts of the offer, property footage, drone
cinematography, editing, AI artistry and custom-mixed soundtracks, because those are what you
compare against another studio's quote.

## The portfolio

The films are the biggest thing on the page. Three vertical tiles, because vertical is the
shape these are shot in and the shape they get watched in, and they start playing as soon as
you reach them.

They play silent, with a sound button on each tile, so the page never makes noise at you
before you ask for it and turning it on is one tap. Each tile carries a South Florida tag,
which is the studio's whole market.

## Capabilities

This section answers two different questions at once. Twelve chips list what the studio can
do, from drone cinematography to virtual staging to multi-platform delivery, which is the
list you check against another quote. Beside them, three cards, Capture, Create and Deliver,
say how a job actually runs.

Keeping both in one section means you can ask what can you do and what happens if I hire you
without scrolling between two answers.

## On a phone

Most of this gets looked at on a phone, often by someone standing in a property. The vertical
tiles are already the right shape for that, so the grid becomes one column and nothing has to
be re-cropped.
