## The problem

Scroll House Studio sells cinematic property films, drone footage and AI visuals. That is a
product nobody can judge from a description. An agent deciding whether to spend money on a
film for a listing wants to see a film, and every second spent reading about the films is a
second the work is not playing.

They also do not need a big site. One studio, one service, one kind of buyer. A services
page, an about page and a portfolio page would have been three places saying overlapping
things, and three chances to land on the wrong one.

## One page, in the order someone decides in

Everything lives on a single page, ordered the way the decision actually goes: see the
work, understand what you get, ask for it. The hero is footage rather than a headline over
a stock photograph, so the first thing anyone judges is the thing they would be buying.

The explanation comes second, once there is something to explain. It is short, and it names
the parts of the offer, cinematography, drone footage, editing, AI artistry and mixed
soundtracks, because those are what a buyer compares against another studio's quote.

## The work carries it

The portfolio is the largest section on the page, deliberately. Vertical tiles, because
that is the shape these films are shot in and the shape they are watched in, and a grid
rather than a carousel, so the range is visible at a glance instead of one at a time.

<img src="/images/projects/scrollhouse-3@720.avif" alt="The portfolio grid, vertical tiles in the shape the films are actually watched in" width="720" height="495" loading="lazy" decoding="async" srcset="/images/projects/scrollhouse-3@720.avif 720w, /images/projects/scrollhouse-3@1440.avif 1440w, /images/projects/scrollhouse-3@2880.avif 2880w" sizes="(max-width: 640px) 100vw, min(100vw, 1440px)" />

A tile opens in place. Nobody is sent to another site or another tab, because a visitor who
leaves to watch something on a video host has left, and the enquiry was on this page.

## What they actually do

Under the work, the capability section splits into capture and create: what happens on site
and what happens after. Two groups rather than a list of twelve services, because the
useful distinction to a buyer is whether someone has to come out to the property.

## On a phone

Most of this is looked at on a phone, often by someone standing in a property. The vertical
tiles are already the right shape for it, so the grid becomes one column and nothing has to
be re-cropped.

## Result

A one page site that shows the films first and turns a scroll into an enquiry, built in
Webflow so the studio can swap the reel as the work changes without coming back to me.
