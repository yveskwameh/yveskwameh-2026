## The problem

Scroll House Studio sells cinematic property films, drone footage and AI visuals. That is
a product nobody can judge from a description, and a page that describes it is already
losing. The site had one job: play the work early, and make the enquiry the obvious next
thing rather than a separate errand.

## One page, not five

Everything lives on a single page. A studio this size does not need a services page, an
about page and a portfolio page that all say some of the same thing. It needs one scroll
that goes from what this looks like, to what you get, to how to ask for it.

## The work carries it

The portfolio is a grid of stills that each open into the real thing. Grouped rather than
listed, so the range is obvious at a glance: exteriors, drone, interiors, the lot. The
headline sits over it rather than above it, because the footage is the argument and the
words are the caption.

## Result

A one page site that shows the films first and turns a scroll into an enquiry, built in
Webflow so the studio can swap the reel as the work changes.
